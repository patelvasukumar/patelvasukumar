P-04Lab project

Arduino Thermometer

Measures room temperature with a thermistor and shows it on a 4-digit 7-segment display, like the one at the top of this page.

C++Arduino MegaSensors
  • Reads an NTC thermistor in a 10 kΩ voltage divider on pin A0 every 100 ms.
  • Averages 10 readings and converts them to °C with the Steinhart–Hart equation.
  • Multiplexes the 4-digit display without blocking delays (millis()), and shows a name banner on a 16×2 LCD.
  • Code split into small header files for the display setup, digit patterns and refresh logic.
